    Research interests

    I am interested in questions that cross technical and human-centered perspectives: how models are trained and evaluated, where they fail, and what people actually need from them.

    Large Language Models

    LLM agents, retrieval-augmented generation, training pipelines, and model evaluation.

    Multimodal AI

    Systems that learn from and reason across multiple modalities, including data generation and curation.

    Human–Computer Interaction

    Interdisciplinary research practices and the relationship between AI systems and human needs.

    Manuscript in preparation · Second author

    Interdisciplinary identities of HCI researchers

    Examining how diverse academic backgrounds shape research approaches and contributions in HCI. Targeting submission to the ACM CHI Conference.

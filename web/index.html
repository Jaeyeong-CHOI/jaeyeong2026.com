01 · Research

Working from both sides of AI.

Training and stress-testing models on one side; studying people, practice, and real needs on the other.

Questions that refuse to sit inside a single discipline are the ones worth staying with.

My research spans human-computer interaction, large language models, and multimodal AI. I design training pipelines, generate and curate data, and run experiments to locate the limits of model behavior.

I also study how researchers and users approach these systems. That interdisciplinary range is not a detour—it is how I look for perspectives a narrower method might miss.
